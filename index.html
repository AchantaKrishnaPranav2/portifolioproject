Behind the lens

Hello, I'm Krishna Pranav

"Photography is how I pause, look closer, and hold on to the feeling of a moment."

I'm based wherever the light is good — mostly outdoors with birds, insects, and the occasional curious dog. My work focuses on wildlife and nature: the patience of waiting for the right moment, the thrill of a Kingfisher holding still, the way a dog's eyes can hold an entire story.

Every photograph in this collection was made with a single camera and natural light — no studio, no staging. Just observation, timing, and a bit of luck.
